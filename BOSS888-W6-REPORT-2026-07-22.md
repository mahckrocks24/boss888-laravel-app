## 2. SARAH — ROOT CAUSE AND FIX
The launch-scope rule already existed, buried at the end of a DELEGATION DISCIPLINE bullet:
"never propose them, never assign them". It said what NOT to do and never said **how to decline**,
so the model filled the gap with plausible framing: *"social media isn't connected here — Marcus
can't publish there."* Two untruths: a REMOVED product described as a DISCONNECTED integration, and
a removed specialist presented as merely unavailable.

Fixes:
- **Prompt** (`ToolSchemaService`): new `HOW TO DECLINE REMOVED CAPABILITIES` block — the exact
  wording to use, an explicit ban on "not connected / not configured / connect an account /
  upgrade / coming soon", a ban on naming the ten removed specialists, and the approved retained
  redirects.
- **`LaunchScopeLanguageGuard`** — deterministic, because a prompt is probabilistic. Rewrites a
  sentence only when it is BOTH about a removed capability AND uses banned framing.
- **`SocialContextProvider` unhooked** from `AgentMeetingEngine` — feeding "0 social posts in 30
  days" into context invited the model to discuss a channel the product does not have.
- **Punctuation normalisation** — models emit U+2019 (') far more than ASCII `'`, so a list written
  as "isn't connected" silently missed the exact sentence it existed to catch.
- **Transactional email protected** — email MARKETING is removed, but password resets, receipts and
  the mailer are retained, so "SMTP is not configured" must survive untouched. Verified both ways.
