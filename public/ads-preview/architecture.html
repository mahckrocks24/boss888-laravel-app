WHAT WE DO

An integrated design practice.

Six disciplines, one studio. We draw every project through architecture, landscape, and interior — so the detail at 1:1 is consistent with the idea at 1:500.

01

Architecture

Private villas, commercial buildings, hospitality and cultural work — from concept through tender and construction administration. Always led personally by one of the three partners.

02

Urban Planning

Masterplans, framework strategies and public-realm design. Two of our masterplans have gone on to win WAF-shortlisted recognition for context-driven, human-scale urbanism.

03

Landscape

In-house landscape architecture — courtyards, roof gardens, desert planting schemes. We design landscape from day one of every project, never as an afterthought.

04

Interior Design

Residential and commercial interiors, including bespoke joinery, lighting schemes and art curation. Our interiors team works alongside the architects — not sequentially — so the building and the room are one thought.

05

Structural Collaboration

Ten-year relationships with our engineering consultants in Dubai and London. Complex structures — long spans, cantilevers, exposed concrete — detailed in the office, not outsourced.

06

Sustainability

LEED and Estidama accredited team members on every job. Passive strategies, local materials, embodied-carbon accounting — treated as design constraints, not add-ons.

HOW WE WORK

Five stages, one conversation.

Every project runs through the same five-stage arc. You're in the room at every stage — our approvals are recorded, not assumed.

Brief

Two to three long conversations, a site visit, a reading of the constraints. We leave with a written brief — the document we all come back to for three years.

Concept

Hand-drawn studies, a physical model, two or three architectural ideas tested against the brief. We present on paper, not screens. Six to ten weeks.

Design

The idea is developed in plan, section and detail. Materials are chosen, mocked up, and touched. Consultants are brought in. Authority approvals filed.

Build

Weekly site presence from a partner — never an intern. We detail in the office, we supervise on site. Shop drawings are checked by the same person who drew the concept.

Deliver

Handover, a 12-month snagging period, and a proper architectural photo shoot. We stay in touch — many of our clients have come back for second and third projects.

THE STUDIO

Twelve people, three partners.

A deliberately small team — one that lets the partners stay close to every drawing and every site visit. Each project is led personally by one partner, with a named architect, a landscape lead, and an interiors lead.

Farah Al-Qassimi

Founding Partner · Architect
RIBA chartered, AA London-trained, 21 years in Gulf architecture. Led our residential work since 2014; sits on the Dubai Design District advisory board.

Rami Haddad

Partner · Head of Urbanism
Harvard GSD, previously at OMA Rotterdam and Foster + Partners. Leads our urban planning practice. Obsessed with how buildings meet the ground and each other.

Leila Mahfouz

Partner · Interior Design
RCA Milan, twelve years with a Milanese joinery workshop before moving to Dubai. Bespoke furniture, lighting and ceramics sit on her desk before they sit in your room.

Daniyal Ahmed

Associate · Landscape
Cambridge-trained landscape architect. Specialises in low-water native planting schemes; his Liwa courtyard at Zariba Desert Lodge halved the brief's irrigation load.
