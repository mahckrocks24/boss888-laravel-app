behind the pass.

Your Visit

From booking
to bill.

01
Reserve

Book online or call — we keep the last twelve seats for walk-ins after 9pm every night.

02
Arrive

Brought to your table by name. The aperitif arrives before the menu — a glass of house cocktail is on us.

03
Eat

The kitchen sends what's running well that night. Speak to your server about allergies, preferences, or anything you'd rather skip.

04
Linger

We don't turn tables — your reservation is yours for the evening. Coffee, digestifs, the cheese trolley.
