This policy explains how LevelUp Growth uses cookies and similar technologies on levelupgrowth.io. For how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use strictly-necessary cookies to run the Service, and — only with your consent — optional analytics cookies.

2. Cookies we use

Cookie / technologyTypePurpose
Session cookieStrictly necessaryKeeps you signed in and secures your session in the app.
Launch-gate preferenceStrictly necessaryRemembers pre-launch site state.
[[OWNER:ANALYTICS_PROVIDER]] analyticsOptional (consent)Aggregate usage measurement. Loads only after consent; nothing transmits until a valid analytics ID is configured.

Strictly-necessary cookies do not require consent. Analytics cookies are loaded only if you accept them.

3. Managing cookies

You can accept or decline optional cookies via our consent banner, and control cookies through your browser settings. Declining optional cookies does not affect core functionality.

4. Consent flow

On first visit, a consent banner offers Accept / Decline for optional analytics. Your choice is stored and respected; analytics scripts are gated behind acceptance. Banner UI to be enabled with analytics — see the Analytics implementation.
