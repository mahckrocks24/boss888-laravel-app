Real questions, answered from your platform state. Short and direct — Aria never pads or pretends.

Aria vs the AI Workforce

Both are AI. They do different jobs — and knowing which is which is the whole point.

🧠 Aria — intelligence layer

The one you talk to.
  • Answers questions about your platform
  • Reads your workspace, agents, tasks, data
  • Points you to the right place
  • Guides — does not execute marketing work

👥 AI Workforce — execution

The team that does the work.
  • Sarah coordinates; specialists execute
  • Runs SEO, content, CRM and creative tasks
  • Works in the background on what you approve
  • You approve — nothing goes live on its own
