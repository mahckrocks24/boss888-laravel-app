Instead of using and paying for multiple separate tools, you get everything in one place with AI that actually executes tasks for you.

AI Agents
On the Growth plan and above, your AI Workforce is led by Sarah (Digital Marketing Manager — strategy + coordination) and includes James (SEO Strategist), Alex (Technical SEO Engineer), Diana (Local SEO Specialist), Ryan (Link Building Specialist), Sofia (International SEO Director), Priya (Content Manager), Nora (Content Strategy Director), Elena (Lead & CRM Manager), and Max (Growth & CRO Director). AI agents are not available on the Free, Starter, or AI Lite plans.
Each agent has a specific role and set of tools they can use. When you start a meeting or dispatch a task (available on Growth plan and above), the relevant agents receive the brief, plan their approach, use their tools (SEO analysis, content generation, CRM updates, etc.), and deliver results. They communicate with each other and coordinate automatically — you just see the finished outputs.
