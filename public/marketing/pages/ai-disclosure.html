Effective date: [[OWNER:EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

We believe you should know exactly how AI works in LevelUp Growth. This disclosure explains what our AI does, what it doesn't, and where you stay in control.

1. Where AI is used

  • Aria — the platform intelligence assistant. Answers questions about your own workspace (your agents, tasks, websites and data) and directs you around the platform.
  • The AI Workforce. Ten specialized AI roles, led by Sarah (Digital Marketing Manager), that research, draft, and organise marketing work — SEO, content, CRM and creative.
  • Content generation. Website copy, articles, images, and video assets are produced with AI.

2. You stay in control

AI output is prepared for your review. Nothing is published to your website or sent to your audience without your approval. The AI does not autonomously post to social platforms, run marketing email campaigns, or make business decisions for you. See Automation for the exact boundaries.

3. AI providers

To generate output, relevant prompts and workspace context are processed by third-party AI providers, currently OpenAI and DeepSeek (and additional creative providers where enabled).
