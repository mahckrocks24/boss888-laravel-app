What we deliberately do not automate

We would rather tell you plainly than let you find out later.

Autonomous social publishing. Your AI prepares social content. It does not post to your accounts for you.
Marketing email campaigns. Newsletters, drip campaigns, nurture campaigns, promotional broadcasts and bulk campaign management are not part of the launch platform.
Autonomous marketing management. No AI decides what your business says to the market without you.

One-to-one and workflow-triggered customer messages — lead follow-ups, booking confirmations, reminders and other operational communication — are automated, and are covered in the first column above.
