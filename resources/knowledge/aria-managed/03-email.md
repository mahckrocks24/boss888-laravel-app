# Email

## Where are our email accounts?
Look at the **Email** page. If it says your email is on your current provider, your mailboxes are still there and keep working exactly as today; they move to LevelUpGrowth on a date agreed with you. Once they have moved, every mailbox is listed on the **Email** page.

## How does moving our email to LevelUpGrowth work?
Three steps, planned with you and done by us. First we agree who needs a mailbox and which addresses stay. Then each person gets a setup link, chooses their own password, and their old mail is copied across. Finally, over one weekend, we switch delivery to LevelUpGrowth; your old provider stays readable for 30 days. No mail is lost and nobody is cut off. Call or write to support to schedule it.

## How do I add a person to our email?
On the **Email** page, choose **Add a person**, enter their name, their new address and an email address they can open today (for example a personal email). We send them a setup link; they choose their own password and their mailbox is ready at that moment.

## Someone did not receive their setup link or it expired. What do I do?
On the **Email** page, find the person (their status says Waiting for setup) and choose **Resend link**. A fresh link is sent and the earlier one stops working. Links work once and expire after 72 hours.

## Can LevelUpGrowth see or reset our passwords?
Nobody else sees a mailbox password, including LevelUpGrowth: each person chooses their own from a one-time link. If someone forgets their password, contact LevelUpGrowth support and we will help them set a new one safely.

## How do I pause or remove a mailbox?
On the **Email** page, choose **Pause** to stop a mailbox for a while without losing any mail, and **Resume** to turn it back on. **Remove** deletes a mailbox and its mail for good, so we confirm every removal with you before it happens.

## How many mailboxes and how much storage do we have?
Your package includes up to 10 mailboxes. The **Email** page shows how many you use and the storage used by each. If you need more, contact support.

## How do I set up email on my phone or computer?
The setup email each person receives explains how to sign in. If a phone or mail app does not connect, contact LevelUpGrowth support and we will walk you through it.

## Does our website still send emails?
Yes. Messages your website sends (for example member notices and event tickets) are sent by LevelUpGrowth's delivery service and are not affected by your mailboxes.
