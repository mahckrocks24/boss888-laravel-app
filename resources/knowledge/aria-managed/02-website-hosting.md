# Website hosting

## Is our website online?
The service status at the top of the portal shows it live: the green **All services operational** button opens the details for your website, email and secure connection. We check your website every 5 minutes and show its uptime for the last 30 days.

## What happens if our website goes down?
Our monitoring checks your website every 5 minutes, day and night. If it stops answering, we are alerted and start fixing it straight away; the service status in the portal shows the problem until it is resolved. You do not need to report it, but you can always call or write to support.

## Is our website backed up?
Yes. Your website's database, uploaded files and settings are backed up every night, encrypted, and copied to a separate location in another region. We keep two weeks of nightly backups and eight weekly ones, and every month we restore a backup on a test copy to prove it works.

## Can you restore our website from a backup?
Yes. If something is deleted by mistake or goes wrong, contact LevelUpGrowth support and say what you need and from when. We restore it from the nightly backups.

## Is our website secure?
Your website is served over a secure connection (the padlock in the browser). The security certificate is renewed automatically; its expiry date is in the service status. We keep the server's software up to date with security updates and protect the site against common attacks.

## Can we change our website ourselves?
Your content (pages, gallery, events, member records and documents) is managed in your own website admin, as before. Changes to how the website is built, its design or new features are done by LevelUpGrowth and quoted separately. The portal is for your hosting, email and billing; it has no file manager on purpose, so nothing on the live site can be broken by mistake.

## Why is there no file manager or control panel?
On purpose. Your website is deployed and maintained by LevelUpGrowth, so a hand-made change could break it or be overwritten. The portal shows only what you need: your email, your package and the health of your services. Everything technical is looked after for you.
