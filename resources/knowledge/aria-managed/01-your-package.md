# Your package

## What does our package include?
Your Managed Hosting package covers four things, all run and looked after by LevelUpGrowth: dedicated website hosting (your own server, kept online and up to date), database management (your member and site data, maintained and tuned), business email hosting on your own domain, and backups and security management (encrypted nightly backups, security certificates and protection). Your package details, term and payment are on the **Billing** page.

## What is not included in our package?
New website features, design changes and custom development are not part of hosting; they are quoted separately at the rate agreed for existing clients. After the free IT support period ends, ongoing IT support can be added at any time. Everything in your package is listed on the **Billing** page; for anything else, call or write to LevelUpGrowth support.

## How long does our package last and when does it renew?
Your package is paid in full for its whole term. The start and end dates and the days left are on the **Billing** page. Nothing more is due until the term ends; we contact you before renewal.

## Do we have IT support?
Yes. Your package includes a free IT support period; the end date and days left are on the **Billing** page. During it, LevelUpGrowth helps with your website, email and the systems behind them. After it ends, IT support can be added at any time.

## Can we upgrade or add services?
Yes. Ongoing IT support and new website features can be added; mailboxes are already unlimited in your package. Call or write to LevelUpGrowth support and we will quote it for you.
