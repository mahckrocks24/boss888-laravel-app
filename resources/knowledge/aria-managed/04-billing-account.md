# Billing, portal and support

## How much did we pay and what is it for?
The **Billing** page shows your package, the amount paid, the date it was paid, the term and the payment record. Your package was paid in full up front for the whole term.

## Where are our invoices and receipts?
On the **Billing** page under **Documents**. When a document shows On request, contact LevelUpGrowth support and we will send it to you.

## Who can use this portal?
Only the people LevelUpGrowth gives a portal sign-in for your organisation. To add or remove someone's portal access, contact support.

## How do I change my portal password?
Open **Account** (or your initials at the top right) and choose **Change password**. Use at least 10 characters with letters and numbers.

## How do I switch between dark and light mode?
At the bottom of the sidebar, or under **Account** > **Appearance**: Dark, Light, or Auto to follow your device.

## How do I contact LevelUpGrowth support?
By phone or email; both are in the **Need a hand?** box in the sidebar and on the **Account** page. Your IT support period and its end date are on the **Billing** page.

## Who is Aria?
Aria is the help assistant for your Managed Hosting package. She answers questions about your website hosting, email, backups, security, billing and this portal, from your package documentation. She cannot change anything on your account and she does not see your mailboxes' contents. For anything she cannot answer, contact LevelUpGrowth support.
