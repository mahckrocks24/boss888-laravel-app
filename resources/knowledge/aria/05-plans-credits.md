# Plans, credits and billing

## What are AI credits?
Credits are the meter for the workforce's work: research, writing, images, video, and every change Arthur makes to a site. Conversation is metered lightly: one credit for every five messages with Sarah, Arthur, Aria or your website chatbot. Every plan from AI Lite includes a monthly allowance that resets each month. Sarah states the cost before anything runs, and every credit, reserved, charged or released, is visible in Billing.

## What does each action cost?
Website: first draft 10 credits; a new page 5; a new section 2; a copy edit 1; a design change (colours, palette, gradient, fonts) 1; moving, aligning, resizing or adding an effect to an element 1; moving or hiding a section 1; a catalogue item 1. Edits you make yourself in the editor (inline text, image or logo replacement, palette, layout, toolbox and drag) are free. Content: an article 2 credits (its featured image 2 more); a social post with its banner 4, posting it 2. Images: a standard image 4 credits (quick 2, high quality 21); editing an image 6; a design 5; a video 28 for 6 seconds (52 for 10 seconds). Publishing an article you already paid to create is free. Chat: 1 credit per 5 messages on every chat surface.

## What plans are there?
Six plans, billed monthly, no contract. Free at $0 with 1 website, 1 user and no workforce; a Free site shows LevelUpGrowth ads. Starter at $19 is Free on your own domain with no ads on your site: 1 website, 1 user, no workforce. AI Lite at $49 with 300 credits a month, 1 website and 1 business, 1 user, Sarah plus 5 specialists. Growth at $99 with 900 credits, 3 websites and 3 businesses, 3 users. Pro at $199 with 2,500 credits, 10 websites and 10 businesses, 5 users, the companion app and priority processing. The companion app is included from Growth up. Agency at $399 with 10,000 credits, 50 websites and 50 businesses, unlimited users, Sarah plus 5 specialists and priority processing. Enterprise and Agency+ are custom. Every plan from AI Lite has the same AI team - Sarah plus 5 specialists you choose; plans differ by credits, websites and team seats. An extra specialist is $20 a month on any plan.

## Which plan is for me?
You want a site and will run the marketing yourself: Free on the LevelUpGrowth subdomain, Starter on your own domain; both include the CRM and calendar, Starter removes the ads, neither includes ongoing AI. You want Sarah and the workforce running growth: AI Lite is the full platform at its smallest capacity; Growth and Pro add sites, credits and seats. You run several businesses or clients: Agency.

## Is there a free trial?
Every new account gets a three-day AI trial with 50 free credits at signup, no card. It starts the moment the account is created, so you can meet Sarah before choosing a plan. During the trial you have the AI Lite tier itself: the website chatbot, every AI feature, Sarah with her five specialists, image and video generation. The trial ends when the 50 credits are spent or three days pass, whichever comes first; the account then continues on Free until you choose a plan.

## How do I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?
From Billing (Advanced) or Account (Basic). Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the next renewal. Cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for, and your data and sites stay accessible for thirty days after cancellation so you can export them.

## How do I buy more credits?
From Billing. Credits show in the sidebar widget with the plan badge; the balance and every movement are listed in Billing.

## What is the refund policy?
Refund terms are being finalised and will be published on the Refunds page before public launch. Until then, write to hello@levelupgrowth.io about any billing question. Cancelling stops future renewals; the plan runs to the end of the paid period.

## Do I own my website and data?
Yes. Sites publish on your subdomain or your own domain, a domain bought here is registered to you, and contacts, leads and the site files can be exported at any time. Nothing is held hostage to a subscription.

## How many team members can I add?
Team members start on Growth: Growth 3 users, Pro 5, Agency unlimited. Free, Starter and AI Lite are for one user, the owner. Invite them from Settings (Advanced).

## What is the companion app?
A mobile companion app included from Growth ($99) and up (Growth, Pro and Agency): approve posts and replies, chat with Sarah and get notifications on the go.
