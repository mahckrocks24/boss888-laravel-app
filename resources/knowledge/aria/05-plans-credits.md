# Plans, credits and billing

## What are AI credits?
Credits are the meter for the workforce's work: research, writing, images, video, and every change Arthur makes to a site. Conversation is metered lightly: one credit for every ten messages with Sarah, Arthur, Aria or your website chatbot. Every plan from AI Lite includes a monthly allowance that resets each month. Sarah states the cost before anything runs, and every credit, reserved, charged or released, is visible in Billing.

## What does each action cost?
Website: first draft 10 credits; a new page 5; a new section 2; a copy edit 1; a design change (colours, palette, gradient, fonts) 1; moving, aligning, resizing or adding an effect to an element 1; moving or hiding a section 1; a catalogue item 1. Edits you make yourself in the editor (inline text, image or logo replacement, palette, layout, toolbox and drag) are free. Images: 2 credits (mini 1, high 4); editing an image 2; a design 5; a video 8. Publishing content you already paid to create is free. Chat: 1 credit per 10 messages on every chat surface.

## What plans are there?
Six plans, billed monthly, no contract. Free at $0 with 1 website, 1 team member and no workforce. Starter at $19 with 1 website, 3 team members, custom domain, social and automations, no workforce. AI Lite at $49 with 50 credits a month, 3 websites, 3 team members, Sarah plus 5 specialists. Growth at $99 with 300 credits, 5 websites, 5 team members. Pro at $199 with 900 credits, 10 websites, 10 team members, the companion app and priority processing. Agency at $399 with 2,500 credits, 25 websites, unlimited team, Sarah plus 10 specialists, white-label and priority processing. Enterprise and Agency+ are custom. An extra specialist is $20 a month ($10 on Agency).
Links: /pricing/

## Which plan is for me?
You want a site and will run the marketing yourself: Free on the LevelUpGrowth subdomain, Starter on your own domain; both include the CRM and calendar, neither includes ongoing AI. You want Sarah and the workforce running growth: AI Lite is the full platform at its smallest capacity; Growth and Pro add sites, credits and seats. You run several businesses or clients: Agency.

## Is there a free trial?
Every new account gets a three-day AI trial with 50 free credits at signup, no card. It starts the moment the account is created, so you can meet Sarah before choosing a plan.

## How do I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?
From Billing (Advanced) or Account (Basic). Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the next renewal. Cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for, and your data and sites stay accessible for thirty days after cancellation so you can export them.

## How do I buy more credits?
From Billing. Credits show in the sidebar widget with the plan badge; the balance and every movement are listed in Billing.

## What is the refund policy?
Refund terms are being finalised and will be published on the Refunds page before public launch. Until then, write to hello@levelupgrowth.io about any billing question. Cancelling stops future renewals; the plan runs to the end of the paid period.

## Do I own my website and data?
Yes. Sites publish on your subdomain or your own domain, a domain bought here is registered to you, and contacts, leads and the site files can be exported at any time. Nothing is held hostage to a subscription.

## How many team members can I add?
Free 1, Starter 3, AI Lite 3, Growth 5, Pro 10, Agency unlimited. Invite them from Settings (Advanced).

## What is the companion app?
A mobile companion included on Pro and Agency for approvals and notifications on the go.

## What is white-label?
On Agency, the client-facing surfaces carry your brand instead of LevelUpGrowth.
