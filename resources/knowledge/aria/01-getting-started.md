# Getting started

## What is LevelUpGrowth?
LevelUpGrowth is one account that holds the assets a business runs on: a website, a CRM, a calendar with online booking, SEO, content, social planning, a website chatbot, creative and video. The work is done by a workforce of named AI specialists led by Sarah, the Digital Marketing Manager. They propose the work and wait for your approval; nothing publishes, posts or sends without it. The app lives at /app/ (your workspace). The company name is written LevelUpGrowth, one word.
Links: /product/

## How do I start?
Sign up at /start/ with your name, email, a password, your business name and industry. No approval step and no verification code: the account opens straight away and you land in the app with Sarah. Describe the business in about two minutes and Arthur, the website builder, builds a site for your industry from your brief. You edit it in place and publish when it is right. Most owners publish on the first day.
Links: /start/, /login/

## Do I need technical skills?
No. You describe the business in plain language and approve what the workforce proposes. You never write code or configure servers.

## How do I log in?
Go to /login/ and enter your email and password. Forgot the password? Use the reset link on the login page; the reset email takes you to /reset-password/.
Links: /login/

## Basic and Advanced views. What is the difference?
The app has two views, switched with the Basic / Advanced toggle at the top of the left sidebar. Basic is the Sarah operating environment: Sarah, Needs attention, Results, Website, Customers, Account and Aria. Advanced reveals every engine and tool: Workspace, Strategy Room, Projects, Command Center, Review Queue, Agents, Messages, Website Wizard, Websites, CRM, SEO, Write, Studio, Social, Chatbot, Calendar, Hosted Sites, Domains, Email Accounts, Reports and History, Billing, Settings and Aria. The choice is remembered on every device you sign in on. If you cannot find a feature, switch to Advanced.

## Where is Aria?
Aria is in the left sidebar in both Basic and Advanced views. She answers questions about the platform: what a feature does, where it lives, what a plan includes, what something costs in credits. She does not change anything in your account. When you want work done, Aria points you to Sarah (for marketing, content, leads and everything the workforce does) or to Arthur (for your website).

## Is there a mobile app?
The web app works on a phone in the browser. The companion app is included on Pro and Agency. The Studio video editor is a desktop experience: review and edit videos on a computer.
