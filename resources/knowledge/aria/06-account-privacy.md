# Account, privacy and AI

## How do I change my name, email or password?
Account in Basic, or Settings in Advanced. Your business details (name, what you do, location, time zone) live there too and Sarah uses them.

## I have more than one business. How do I switch?
Account (Basic) lists your businesses when there is more than one; switching is always an explicit click and changes the business you work in everywhere: Sarah, websites and data.

## How is AI used and what are its limits?
Sarah and the specialists research, draft and organise marketing work; Arthur builds and edits sites; the Studio generates images and video; Aria answers questions about the platform. AI output is prepared for your review and can be wrong; verify anything important before publishing. The workforce works inside your account and does not log in to third-party tools on your behalf. Prompts and workspace context are processed by third-party AI providers to produce output.
Links: /legal/ai-disclosure/

## Can Aria see my customers' data or change my account?
Aria reads only your plan name and credit balance to answer plan questions, and answers everything else from the platform documentation. She never changes settings, spends credits on work, or executes anything. Work goes through Sarah or Arthur with your approval.

## Where are the legal documents?
Terms of Service at /legal/terms/, Privacy at /legal/privacy/, Cookies at /legal/cookies/, Refunds and Billing at /legal/refunds/, AI Disclosure at /legal/ai-disclosure/. Some company details are marked "to be confirmed before launch" until public launch.
Links: /legal/terms/, /legal/privacy/

## How do I contact support?
Write to hello@levelupgrowth.io, or ask Sarah in the app. Aria answers platform questions here at any time.

## Why can I not generate video or use a feature?
Most limits are plan limits: SEO, content, images, video, the chatbot and the workforce start on AI Lite; custom domains, social and automations on Starter. Check Billing and upgrade, or ask Aria what your plan includes.

## The app looks different from what I expected. Why?
You may be in Basic view. Switch to Advanced with the toggle at the top of the sidebar to see every tool. Dark, light and auto appearance are at the bottom of the sidebar.
