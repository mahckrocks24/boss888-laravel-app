# Affiliate Program

## Do you have an affiliate program?
Yes, the LevelUpGrowth Affiliate Program. Creators, vloggers, bloggers, newsletter writers, consultants and agencies can apply to share LevelUpGrowth with their own code and earn. Every application is read by a person, usually within 2 working days. Joining is free and you do not need a LevelUpGrowth plan.
Links: /affiliates/

## How much do affiliates earn?
Each business that signs up with an affiliate's code carries a share of what it pays: 20% of a monthly plan for its first 6 monthly payments, 15% of a yearly plan (once), and 10% of new domain registrations in its first 6 months. A code with no discount keeps the whole share for the affiliate. For example, one business on Pro at $199 a month earns the affiliate $39.80 a month for 6 payments.
Links: /affiliates/

## Can an affiliate give my audience a discount?
Yes. In the affiliate portal an affiliate creates codes and splits each share between a discount for the customer and their own commission, in whole percent, with one bar per product. The standard code is 5% off for the customer and 15% for the affiliate on monthly plans. A 10% code gives 10% off and 10% to the affiliate. Discount plus commission always equals the share.
Links: /affiliates/

## I have an affiliate or promotion code. Where do I enter it?
On the sign-up page, under "Have a code?". Codes are for new customers and apply before the first payment; one code per account. On monthly plans the discount applies to the first 6 monthly payments.
Links: /start/

## When and how are affiliates paid?
Each commission is held for 30 days in case of a refund, then becomes ready to pay. Ready balances are paid on the 15th of each month, by PayPal or Wise, once they reach $50; smaller amounts roll over. Refunded or disputed payments take back the matching commission.
Links: /legal/affiliates/

## What does not earn a commission?
Free trials until their first payment, credit top-ups, domain renewals, the $1 first-year domain offer, and the affiliate's own account or any business they own or belong to.
Links: /legal/affiliates/

## Do affiliates get a tracking link?
No. The Affiliate Program works with codes only: a business counts for an affiliate when it signs up with that affiliate's code. There are no tracking links and no tracking cookies. Affiliates who want to see which video or post works make a different code for each one.
Links: /affiliates/

## Where do affiliates sign in?
At levelupgrowth.io/affiliates/portal, with their LevelUpGrowth login. The portal shows sign-ups, paying businesses, earnings by code, codes and payouts. It never shows a referred business's work, customers or website.
Links: /affiliates/portal
