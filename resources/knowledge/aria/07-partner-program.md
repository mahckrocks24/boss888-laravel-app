# Partner program

## Do you have an affiliate or partner program?
Yes. Creators, vloggers, bloggers, newsletter writers, consultants and agencies can apply to share LevelUpGrowth and earn. Every application is read by a person, usually within 2 working days. Joining is free and you do not need a LevelUpGrowth plan.
Links: /partners/

## How much do partners earn?
Each business a partner brings carries a share of what it pays: 20% of a monthly plan for its first 6 monthly payments, 15% of a yearly plan (once), and 10% of new domain registrations in its first 6 months. With a link alone the partner keeps the whole share. For example, one business on Pro at $199 a month earns the partner $39.80 a month for 6 payments.
Links: /partners/

## Can a partner give my audience a discount?
Yes. In the partner portal a partner creates codes and splits each share between a discount for the customer and their own commission, in whole percent, with one bar per product. The standard code is 5% off for the customer and 15% for the partner on monthly plans. A 10% code gives 10% off and 10% to the partner. Discount plus commission always equals the share.
Links: /partners/

## I have a partner or promotion code. Where do I enter it?
On the sign-up page, under "Have a code?". A partner's link can fill it in for you. Codes are for new customers and apply before the first payment; one code per account. On monthly plans the discount applies to the first 6 monthly payments.
Links: /start/

## When and how are partners paid?
Each commission is held for 30 days in case of a refund, then becomes ready to pay. Ready balances are paid on the 15th of each month to the partner's bank account once they reach $50; smaller amounts roll over. Refunded or disputed payments take back the matching commission.
Links: /legal/partners/

## What does not earn a commission?
Free trials until their first payment, credit top-ups, domain renewals, the $1 first-year domain offer, and the partner's own account or any business they own or belong to.
Links: /legal/partners/

## How long does a partner link count?
Anyone who signs up within 60 days of clicking a partner link counts for that partner. A code typed at sign-up takes priority over any link. Once the business pays for the first time, the referral is fixed.
Links: /partners/

## Where do partners sign in?
At levelupgrowth.io/partners/portal, with their LevelUpGrowth login. The portal shows clicks, sign-ups, paying businesses, earnings by link and video, codes and payouts. It never shows a referred business's work, customers or website.
Links: /partners/portal
