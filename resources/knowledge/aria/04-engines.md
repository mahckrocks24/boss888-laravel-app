# Engines and tools

## What does the SEO suite do?
It audits your site (crawl issues, metadata gaps, speed, structure, schema), tracks the keywords you care about (25 on AI Lite, 100 on Growth, Pro and Agency) and hands fixes to the workforce: technical issues to Alex, local signals to Diana, links to Ryan, content briefs to Priya. Rankings depend on your market; the platform shows movement and never promises positions. Included from AI Lite. Open SEO in Advanced, or ask Sarah.
Links: /product/seo/

## How does content writing work?
Priya and the content team write articles from keyword briefs and your brand notes. Every article carries its title tag, description, focus keyword, image alt text and structured data, and passes Sarah's review before it reaches you for approval. Publish from the review, or tell Sarah "publish". Included from AI Lite. Open Write in Advanced.
Links: /product/content/

## How does social work?
Marcus plans thirty days at a time; Maya, Zara, Tyler and Zoe write per channel; every post waits in the queue for your approval. Publishing goes through the channel connections you authorise; until a channel is connected, posts are prepared and scheduled but not sent. Included from Starter. Open Social in Advanced.
Links: /product/social/

## What is the CRM?
Every lead from your site forms, the chatbot and booking requests lands in one pipeline with its source and notes. Stages, tags, follow-up rules and the whole history on the record. Import contacts by CSV and export them at any time. Included on every plan. Open Customers in Basic or CRM in Advanced.
Links: /product/crm/

## How does the calendar and booking work?
Customers book from a booking section on your site or a shareable link. Bookings land on your calendar and in the CRM, confirmations and reminders go out, and the rules you set handle the follow-up. Payment collection is not part of booking. Included on every plan. Open Calendar in Advanced.
Links: /product/calendar/

## What is the website chatbot?
A chatbot trained on the documents and pages you give it, answering on the domains you allow, capturing names, phone numbers and booking requests into the CRM. It answers what it knows and says so when it does not. Knowledge documents per plan: 25 on AI Lite, 50 on Growth, 100 on Pro, unlimited on Agency. Messages per month: 500, 1,000, 5,000, unlimited. Included from AI Lite. Open Chatbot in Advanced.
Links: /product/chatbot/

## What is the Studio?
The Studio makes images, ad creatives and video. Images follow your brand notes and the subject you ask for, in square, story, banner and hero sizes, and are stored in your media library. Video is scripted first (Chris writes the script for your approval), assembled from your brand, your images and generated scenes, and reviewed in the Studio editor before use. The video editor is a desktop experience. Credits for a failed render are returned automatically. Included from AI Lite. Open Studio in Advanced, or ask Sarah for an image.
Links: /product/creative/, /product/video/

## What is Automation?
Deterministic rules that run themselves inside your account: when a lead arrives, when a booking is made, when a form is submitted, do this (notify, tag, change stage, remind). Rules do not use credits and never send bulk marketing email. Included from Starter; rules live in the CRM.
Links: /product/automation/

## Where are my generated images?
In the Studio under Results, in your media library, and inline in the Sarah chat when Sarah makes them.

## Why does the image not show my exact logo or text?
AI image models cannot reproduce a specific logo or exact lettering; it comes out garbled. Upload your real logo in the editor instead.

## Where do I see performance and analytics?
Results in Basic, or Reports and History in Advanced. Numbers shown are measured from your own connected data; nothing is inferred.
