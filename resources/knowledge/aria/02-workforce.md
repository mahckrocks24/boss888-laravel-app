# The AI workforce

## Who is Sarah?
Sarah is the Digital Marketing Manager. You talk to her in the Sarah view (the Basic home) or in Messages. She turns your goals into a plan, prices each job in credits, briefs the specialists, reviews every finished task for alignment with the brief, complete metadata and provenance, and brings the work to you for approval. Work that fails her review returns to draft and is never counted as done.

## Who is Arthur?
Arthur is the website builder. Describe the business and he builds a multi-page site for your industry with your copy, colours and images, hosted on a LevelUpGrowth subdomain with SSL. After that he edits on request in plain language: change copy, recolour one element or a whole section, add a section or a page, move, align or resize elements, add opacity, shadows or a glow, manage listings, services or menu items. You reach Arthur from the Website view (Basic) or Websites (Advanced), or ask Sarah, who hands website work to him.
Links: /product/website-builder/

## Can I talk to the specialists directly?
You talk to Sarah, who briefs and manages the specialists, and to Arthur about your website. Aria answers questions about the platform itself.

## Does anything publish without me?
No. Every publish, send or post waits for your approval in the Review Queue (Needs attention in Basic), and Sarah's review runs before that.

## What is the Strategy Room?
The Strategy Room (Advanced) is a planning session with Sarah and the specialists about your goals; it produces a plan you approve before any work runs. Included from AI Lite.

## What is the Command Center and the Review Queue?
Command Center (Advanced) shows the tasks the workforce is running and has finished. Review Queue lists everything waiting for your approval. In Basic the same items appear under Needs attention and Results.

## How do I ask Sarah to do something?
Open Sarah and write what you want in plain language: "write a blog post about spring menus", "plan this month's social posts", "add a testimonials section to my site". She states the cost in credits, asks for approval when the work spends them, and reports back when it is done.
