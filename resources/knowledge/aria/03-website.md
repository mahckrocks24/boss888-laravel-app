# Website and hosting

## How do I build a website?
In Basic open Website, in Advanced open Website Wizard or Websites, or simply tell Sarah "build my website". Arthur asks about the business, the style and your colours, then builds the site. A first draft costs 10 credits. Review it, edit anything, and publish.
Links: /product/website-builder/

## How do I edit my website myself?
Open the site in the editor (Website in Basic, Websites in Advanced). Click any heading, paragraph, image or button on the page and change it: double-click text to edit it inline, replace an image or logo, change the palette, switch the layout, drag an element within its section, or use the small toolbox that appears on a selected element to align, resize, and set opacity, shadow or glow. Edits you make yourself are free. Toggle between the phone and desktop preview at the top of the editor.

## How do I ask Arthur to change something?
In the editor, select an element or a section and tell Arthur in the chat panel: "make this button white", "give the listings section a purple and pink gradient", "move the photo above the text", "add a booking form section", "add a pricing page". Arthur names the change and the credits before he does it. He works on the selected element or section; when you name a different one, he asks which you mean.

## What can Arthur add to a site?
Pages (5 credits each) such as About, Services, Contact, Pricing, Blog, Gallery, Booking. Sections (2 credits each): booking or appointment form, events calendar, pricing table, FAQ, testimonials, team, photo gallery, stats strip, features grid, services grid, map, trust badges, contact form, video, call-to-action banner, trip planner quiz. Copy edits and design changes are 1 credit. Element moves, alignment, resizing and effects are 1 credit each. Catalogue items (listings, services, menu items, plans, rooms, vehicles, programmes, events) are 1 credit each to add or change.

## Is hosting included?
Yes. Every site is published on a LevelUpGrowth subdomain with SSL, on infrastructure the platform operates, at no extra cost, on every plan. Managed hosting on your own domain with a dedicated runtime is a separate service that is coming soon and is not included in current plans.
Links: /product/hosting/

## Can I connect my own domain?
Yes, from Starter upward. Buy a domain from your account (Domains in Advanced) or connect one you already own from the site settings; SSL is provisioned automatically and the registration is in your name. Transfers of existing domains are handled by request.
Links: /product/domains/

## What does a domain cost?
The price is shown at search time in Domains and includes the first year. Auto-renew is switched on or off from the domain itself.

## Can I export my website and data?
Yes. Export the site's static files at any time from the editor. Contacts and leads export as CSV from the CRM. A domain bought here is registered to you.

## Do forms on my site reach me?
Yes. Every contact form, booking request and chatbot conversation on a site built here creates or updates a lead in the CRM with its source. Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Meta and TikTok pixel ids can be added from the editor for free.

## Can I sell on my site?
A store or a paid listing can take payment on your own Stripe account, connected from the site settings; the platform does not hold the money.

## Is business email available?
Business email on your domain (mailboxes, aliases, forwarders) is coming soon and is not included in current plans. Connecting a domain here does not change where your mail is hosted; Gmail, Outlook or your current provider keep working.
Links: /product/business-email/
