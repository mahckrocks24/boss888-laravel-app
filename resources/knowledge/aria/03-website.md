# Website and hosting

## How do I build a website?
In Basic open Website, in Advanced open Website Wizard or Websites, or simply tell Sarah "build my website". Arthur asks about the business, the style and your colours, then builds the site. A first draft costs 10 credits. Review it, edit anything, and publish.
Links: /product/website-builder/

## How do I edit my website myself?
Open the site in the editor (Website in Basic, Websites in Advanced). Click any heading, paragraph, image or button on the page and change it: double-click text to edit it inline, replace an image or logo, change the palette, switch the layout, drag an element within its section, or use the small toolbox that appears on a selected element to move, align, resize, add effects (opacity, shadow, glow), link it, and for a photo choose how it fits its frame, which part stays in view, its width, or crop it. Add page and Add section open pickers of ready templates in the site's own style. Publishing keeps you in the editor. Edits you make yourself are free. Toggle between the phone and desktop preview at the top of the editor.

## How do I link a button or a line of text?
In the editor click the button, text or logo, choose Link in its toolbox and enter where it should go: a page of your site, a section (for example the contact form), a web address, a phone number or an email address; tick "open in a new tab" if you want that. Leave the field empty to remove a link. Free. You can also ask Arthur: "link the Book Now button to the booking section".

## How do I crop a photo or change how it fits?
Click the photo, open Picture in its toolbox: choose Fill (the photo fills its frame, edges may be trimmed) or Whole (the entire photo shows), pick which part stays in view (top, bottom, left, right or a corner), set its width with the slider, or choose Crop to cut the picture yourself. Free. Arthur does the same from chat: "make the hero photo fill the frame", "keep the faces at the top in view".

## How do I open another page while editing? Long-press a link or menu item
Press and hold (long-press) any link or menu item in the editor. A small menu offers: go to that page (it opens in the editor, with a Home pill to come back), scroll to that section, open it in a new tab, or edit the link. A normal click selects the element instead of navigating, so nothing happens by accident.

## How do I ask Arthur to change something?
In the editor, select an element or a section and tell Arthur in the chat panel: "make this button white", "give the listings section a purple and pink gradient", "move the photo above the text", "add a booking form section", "add a pricing page". Arthur names the change and the credits before he does it. He works on the selected element or section; when you name a different one, he asks which you mean.

## What can Arthur add to a site?
Pages (5 credits each) such as About, Services, Contact, Pricing, Blog, Gallery, Booking. Sections (2 credits each): booking or appointment form, events calendar, pricing table, FAQ, testimonials, team, photo gallery, stats strip, features grid, services grid, map, trust badges, contact form, video, call-to-action banner, trip planner quiz. Copy edits and design changes are 1 credit. Element moves, alignment, resizing, effects, links and photo fit (fill, whole, focus, width, crop) are 1 credit each. Catalogue items (listings, services, menu items, plans, rooms, vehicles, programmes, events) are 1 credit each to add or change.

## Can Sarah work on several websites at once?
Yes. In one message, name a website per piece ("a sourdough article for the Bakery and a latte article for the Cafe") and each piece goes to its own site; or say "for each website", "on all my sites" or "both" and the same work runs on every site you have (or the ones you named). Sarah names the sites in her reply. Each piece belongs to one website, nothing goes live without your approval, and if she cannot tell which site a piece is for she asks instead of guessing.

## Is there a tour of the editor? How do I see it again?
Yes. The first time you open a website in the editor, a short guided tour walks you through it: selecting and editing text, the element toolbox, Arthur, adding pages and sections, layouts and colours, the catalogue (when your site has one), Undo and Versions, Preview, and Publish. To see it again, press the ? button in the editor's toolbar. It works on a phone and on a desktop.

## Is hosting included?
Yes. Every site is published on a LevelUpGrowth subdomain with SSL, on infrastructure the platform operates, at no extra cost, on every plan. Managed hosting on your own domain with a dedicated runtime is a separate service that is coming soon and is not included in current plans.
Links: /product/hosting/

## Can I connect my own domain?
Yes, from Starter upward. Buy a domain from your account (Domains in Advanced) or connect one you already own from the site settings; SSL is provisioned automatically and the registration is in your name. Transfers of existing domains are handled by request.
Links: /product/domains/

## What does a domain cost?
The price is shown at search time in Domains and includes the first year. Auto-renew is switched on or off from the domain itself.

## Can I export my website and data?
Yes. Export the site's static files at any time from the editor. Contacts and leads export as CSV from the CRM. A domain bought here is registered to you.

## Do forms on my site reach me?
Yes. Every contact form, booking request and chatbot conversation on a site built here creates or updates a lead in the CRM with its source. Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Meta and TikTok pixel ids can be added from the editor for free.

## Can I sell on my site?
A store or a paid listing can take payment on your own Stripe account, connected from the site settings; the platform does not hold the money.

## Is business email available?
Business email on your domain (mailboxes, aliases, forwarders) is coming soon and is not included in current plans. Connecting a domain here does not change where your mail is hosted; Gmail, Outlook or your current provider keep working.
Links: /product/business-email/
